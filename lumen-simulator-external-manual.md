# LUMEN Germany Market Entry Simulator — External User Manual

## Purpose

Use the simulator to test how price, channel allocation, and launch month affect expected revenue, contribution, acceptance, payback, and LTV:CAC. It is a decision aid, not a guaranteed forecast.

## Recommended starting scenario

- Price: **€2.19 per can**
- Channel mix: **45% DTC Online / 35% Gym & Office / 20% Retail/Grocery**
- Timing: **June soft launch, July scale-up**
- Cities: **Berlin and Munich first; Hamburg next**

## How to use it

1. Select a price point.
2. Enter the proposed percentage for each channel. Allocations are normalized if they do not total 100%.
3. Select a launch month.
4. Click **Update simulation**.
5. Review contribution, acceptance, payback, and LTV:CAC together.
6. Change one input at a time when comparing scenarios.

## Reading the outputs

At €2.19, the case data estimates 51.7% acceptance and channel contribution of €1.16/unit for DTC, €1.13 for Gym & Office, and €0.63 for Retail/Grocery. Retail is useful for reach and trial, while DTC and Gym/Office are stronger economic channels.

The timing chart compares seasonal demand and competitor promotions. July is the demand peak at index 138; January has zero modeled promotions; June is the recommended compromise at index 132 with one promotion.

Revenue measures scale, but contribution is better for comparing channel designs. Payback shows how quickly marketing investment is recovered. LTV:CAC is directional; the planning benchmark is approximately 3:1. Do not approve a scenario on LTV:CAC alone if payback is too slow.

## Decision workflow

1. Run the recommended base case.
2. Test €1.79 and €2.59 while keeping channels and timing constant.
3. Compare the recommended channel mix with retail-heavy and DTC-heavy alternatives.
4. Compare June, July, and January to understand demand versus competition.
5. Choose the scenario that remains credible if acceptance or demand is lower than expected.

## Ideal conclusion

The evidence-backed recommendation is a **€2.19 June launch**, led by **DTC Online and Gym & Office**, beginning in **Berlin and Munich**. Position LUMEN around clean-label afternoon focus, natural caffeine, low sugar, credible adaptogens, and repeatable taste. Use Retail/Grocery selectively for sampling and visibility.

## Limitations

- There is no German historical sales data; Germany is estimated from survey, benchmark, and comparable-market inputs.
- Survey respondents are synthetic and directional.
- City shares are illustrative market-sizing assumptions.
- Simulator results are estimates, not guaranteed commercial outcomes.
