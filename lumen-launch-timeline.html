Lead launch

Clean Focus

Adaptogen-led, coffee-replacement drink for Urban Wellness Professionals. Start with DTC and selected grocery in Berlin, Hamburg, and Munich.

Best fit: 9.1/10 purchase intent, 3.6/10 price sensitivity, 7.6 purchases/month. Quotes favor clean ingredients and adaptogens; taste must avoid “medicine.”
Gate: Repeat-rate and taste acceptance prove the premium proposition before wider distribution.
Second wave

Performance Refresh

Great-tasting functional energy for Fitness & Gym-Goers. Pilot gym/office placement with a clear performance reason to earn shelf space.

8.0/10 purchase intent; Gym & Office has 37 surveyed fitness respondents. Quote signal: outperform VoltFit on taste, not packaging alone.
Gate: Demonstrate velocity per gym and performance/taste preference against VoltFit.
Channel expansion

Station Trial

Single-SKU, grab-and-go test at station kiosks; lead with an unmistakable taste cue and easy first trial.

Commuters show 6.7/10 intent, but quotes say kiosk availability and taste determine adoption; habit is the core barrier.
Gate: Kiosk conversion and repeat purchase justify a permanent commuter line.
Value expansion

Exam-Period Value Pack

Limited multi-pack or promotion for Students & Budget-Conscious shoppers, timed around exams and sold through grocery/DTC.

Students have the lowest intent (5.5/10) and highest price sensitivity (7.9/10). Quotes explicitly ask for exam-period promotions and reject €2.50/can.
